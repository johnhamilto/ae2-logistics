# ME Tracer Terminal

<ItemImage id="tracer_terminal" scale="2" />

The oscilloscope for [signals](signals.md): every channel on the network with live
values, refreshed once a second.

- Click a channel for a **five-minute sparkline** (one sample per second,
  recorded server-side) with min/max/now labels.
- Scroll the list with the mouse wheel; up to 64 channels are charted per network.
