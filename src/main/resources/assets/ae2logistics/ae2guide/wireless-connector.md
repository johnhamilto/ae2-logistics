# ME Wireless Connector

<ItemImage id="wireless_connector" scale="4" />

A piece of smart cable whose run is the air: every color-compatible connector in
mutual range gets a real grid connection, exactly as if cable were laid between
them. No linking UI, no frequencies, no GUI - **placement is the pairing and color
is the configuration**.

- **Fluix connects to anything.** Dyed one of the 16 colors, a connector links only
  to its own color (and fluix ones) - AE2's cable color rule, in the air. Two
  neighboring fluix networks in range will fuse, exactly like touching cables;
  color them apart.
- **Right-click with a dye** to recolor in place. A **Fluix Crystal** resets to
  fluix.
- **16-block base range**, same dimension only. Click **Wireless Boosters** onto
  the connector to extend it on AE2's access-point range curve (up to 8); an empty
  hand pops one back out. Reach is mutual - the shorter range of the pair must
  cover the distance.
- **Channel-honest.** The link takes no channel of its own and passes up to 8,
  like smart cable, and routing prefers every wired path first: a wireless link
  parallel to a real trunk carries nothing, and the air only serves what cable
  cannot reach.
