# AE2 Logistics

AE2 gives you a storage plane (what you have) and an execution plane (what you craft).
AE2 Logistics adds the missing **control plane**: numbers your network can carry, logic
that computes with them, and patterns that match ingredients flexibly.

Three ideas, three pages:

- **Signals** — named numeric channels that live on your network, show up in terminals,
  and drive Level Emitters.
- **Logic Parts** — small cable parts that read and write signals: thresholds,
  hysteresis, arithmetic, timers, sensors.
- **Adaptive Patterns** — processing patterns that accept "any #planks" or "any damage"
  instead of one exact item.

Everything is server-computed, deterministic, and designed to sleep when idle.
