# Config Blueprint

<ItemImage id="config_blueprint" scale="2" />

Region-scale settings capture: photograph the configuration of every device in a
box, apply it to a rebuilt copy.

- Click corner one, then corner two (up to 4096 blocks): captures every AE2-based
  device and every AE2 Logistics device in the box - types, sides, and full
  settings.
- **Sneak-click the minimum corner** of a rebuilt region to apply to
  type-matching devices. Sneak-use in air to clear the blueprint.
- Pairs with the [ME Config Terminal](config-terminal.md) for single-device
  copy/paste and fleet-wide paste.
