# Regulus Crystal

<ItemImage id="regulus_crystal" scale="2" />

The mod's themed resource: it prices the control tier (guarded providers,
schedulers, query devices, the cores).

Made **in-world**: drop a Charged Certus Quartz Crystal, Redstone, and Glowstone
Dust together into water and they fuse into two Regulus Crystals.
